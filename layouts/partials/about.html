About Me

I'm a final-year Data Science student at RMIT University, Melbourne, with a passion for turning messy, complex data into clear and meaningful stories.

My approach to data science is a lot like Lego — I love taking many small, seemingly unrelated pieces and building them into something useful and beautiful. Whether it's a Power BI dashboard, a machine learning model, or a data pipeline, I care deeply about the craft.

When I'm not wrangling data, you'll find me exploring Melbourne, learning something new, or thinking about how to make complex ideas simple.
